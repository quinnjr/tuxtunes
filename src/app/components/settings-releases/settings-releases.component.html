New From Your Artists

The New Releases view lists recent albums, EPs and singles by the artists in your library. Release data comes from MusicBrainz and covers from the Cover Art Archive. Each card opens the release on Qobuz.

Qobuz

Without an app ID, a card opens a Qobuz search for the artist and title. With one, TuxTunes matches each release to its Qobuz album, so the card opens the album page and shows Qobuz's cover. Qobuz does not hand out app IDs to the public, so use one you are entitled to.
